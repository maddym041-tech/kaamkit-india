YOUR PRIVACY

Your files should stay yours.

PDF and image processing in this starter version happens in your browser using open-source libraries loaded from a third-party CDN. Files are not intentionally sent to a KaamKit backend. Do not use this site for highly sensitive documents until you have independently reviewed the code, dependencies and hosting configuration.
